Brand identity

Ardoise

Your tab, on chain. Every payment is one line on the slate, borrowed against your bitcoin without selling it.

Design concept

A slate, one line per payment

Why a slate. Ardoise is French for the slate a café keeps for running tabs. Ardoise works the same way: every payment is one debt line, borrowed on Arc at the best rate against your bitcoin, which is never sold.

Why ledger lines. The three bars are entries on the tab, written in chalk, each shorter than the last. The dot is the payment being written now. The mark reads as a statement at a glance and holds at 16 px.

Why these colors. Black slate and white chalk are what a real ardoise looks like: calm, premium, nothing loud. Bitcoin orange appears only once, on the payment being written, so it says what you pay with and draws the eye to the action. A dark gray frame keeps the slate visible on dark backgrounds.

Why Geist. The app already uses Geist. In semibold with tight tracking, the wordmark stays crisp on a payment screen and lets the mark carry the idea.
